Smart Watch Buying Guide: What to Look For Before Buying

Looking for a smart watch? This guide explains the main things to consider before choosing one, including compatibility, display, battery life, fitness features, notifications, comfort and overall value.

A smart watch can be useful for checking notifications, tracking activities, monitoring workouts and staying connected without constantly reaching for your phone.

Different smart watches offer different features, so it is important to consider how you plan to use yours before making a purchase.

1. Check Compatibility

Before buying a smart watch, check whether it is compatible with your smartphone and the operating system you use.

  • Check compatibility with your smartphone.
  • Check the required mobile app.
  • Check whether all advertised features work with your device.
  • Consider compatibility if you plan to change phones later.

2. Display and Controls

The display is one of the features you will interact with most often. Consider the screen size, readability and how easy it is to navigate.

  • Check the display size.
  • Consider readability in different lighting conditions.
  • Check whether the watch uses a touchscreen, physical buttons or both.
  • Consider whether the interface is easy for you to use.

3. Battery Life

Battery life can make a big difference in everyday use. Check the manufacturer's stated battery life and consider how frequently you are comfortable charging the watch.

Actual battery performance can vary depending on features such as display brightness, notifications, activity tracking and other settings.

4. Fitness and Activity Features

If fitness tracking is important to you, check which activities and measurements the smart watch supports.

  • Check the activity tracking features.
  • Look at the supported workout modes.
  • Check which health or fitness measurements are available.
  • Consider whether the features match your activities.

Features and measurements can vary between models, so always check the specific product information before purchasing.

5. Notifications and Connectivity

Many smart watches can display notifications from a connected smartphone. Think about which notifications you actually want to receive on your wrist.

  • Check notification support.
  • Check Bluetooth connectivity.
  • Check whether calls or messages are supported if you need them.
  • Consider whether the watch requires your phone nearby for certain features.

6. Comfort and Design

A smart watch is normally worn for long periods, so comfort and design should not be overlooked.

  • Consider the size and weight.
  • Check the strap design.
  • Consider whether the watch fits comfortably on your wrist.
  • Choose a design that suits your everyday clothing and activities.

7. Price and Value

Consider the features you actually need instead of choosing a smart watch based only on the number of features listed.

Compare compatibility, battery life, fitness features, display, comfort and connectivity with the price to decide whether the product matches your needs.

Smart Watch Quick Checklist

  • ☐ Compatible with your smartphone
  • ☐ Suitable display and controls
  • ☐ Battery life meets your needs
  • ☐ Fitness features match your activities
  • ☐ Notifications work as expected
  • ☐ Suitable Bluetooth/connectivity features
  • ☐ Comfortable size and strap
  • ☐ Price matches the features you need
