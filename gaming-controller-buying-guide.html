Gaming Controller Buying Guide

Looking for a gaming controller? This guide explains the important things to consider before buying, including compatibility, connection, controls, comfort, battery or power requirements and value.

1. Check Compatibility

Compatibility should be one of the first things you check before purchasing a gaming controller.

Check whether the controller supports the device or gaming platform you plan to use, such as PC, console, mobile or another supported device.

Always check the manufacturer's product specifications for the exact supported devices.

2. Choose the Right Connection

Gaming controllers can connect to devices in different ways.

  • Wired USB connection
  • Bluetooth connection
  • Wireless connection using a dedicated receiver

Consider whether you prefer a wired setup or the freedom of a wireless connection.

3. Check the Controls

Look at the buttons, analog sticks, triggers and other controls included with the controller.

Depending on the games you play, features such as additional buttons, vibration feedback or adjustable controls may be useful.

Check the exact features listed for the controller before buying.

4. Consider Comfort

A controller should feel comfortable during the amount of time you normally spend gaming.

  • Check the shape and size.
  • Consider the weight.
  • Look at the button layout.
  • Make sure the controls are easy to reach.

If possible, consider how the controller's design fits your hands and gaming style.

5. Check Battery or Power Requirements

Wireless controllers normally require a power source. Check whether the controller uses rechargeable batteries, replaceable batteries or another power system.

For wired controllers, check the cable length and connection type to make sure they work with your gaming setup.

6. Check Compatibility With Your Games

A controller may support your device but still have limitations with particular games or software.

Check the game's controller support and the manufacturer's information before purchasing, especially when using a controller on PC or mobile.

7. Consider Build Quality

Look at the materials, buttons, triggers, analog sticks and overall construction of the controller.

Customer reviews can provide additional information about how the controller performs during normal use.

8. Compare Price and Value

Compare the controller's compatibility, connection type, controls, comfort, power requirements and additional features with its price.

The right controller depends on your gaming setup and the features that are important to you.

Quick Gaming Controller Checklist

  • ✔ Check compatibility with your device.
  • ✔ Choose the connection type you need.
  • ✔ Check the buttons and controls.
  • ✔ Consider comfort, size and weight.
  • ✔ Check battery or power requirements.
  • ✔ Confirm compatibility with your games.
  • ✔ Check build quality and customer reviews.
  • ✔ Compare features and value before buying.
