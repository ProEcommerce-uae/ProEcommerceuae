Gaming Headset Buying Guide: What to Look For Before Buying

Looking for a gaming headset? This guide explains the main things to consider before choosing one, including compatibility, comfort, audio quality, microphone, connection, controls and overall value.

A gaming headset can make gaming more convenient by combining headphones and a microphone in one device. It can also be useful for voice chat, watching videos and listening to music.

Before buying a gaming headset, consider the device you will use it with and the features that matter most to you.

1. Check Device Compatibility

Start by checking whether the gaming headset is compatible with your gaming platform or device.

  • Check compatibility with your gaming console.
  • Check compatibility with PC if needed.
  • Check compatibility with mobile devices if you plan to use them.
  • Check the connection type required by the headset.

2. Comfort

Gaming sessions can sometimes last for hours, so comfort is an important consideration.

  • Consider the headset's weight.
  • Check the ear-cup design.
  • Look at the headband padding.
  • Consider whether the headset can be adjusted comfortably.

A comfortable fit can be especially important if you plan to use the headset for long gaming sessions.

3. Audio Quality

Audio quality can affect your experience when playing games, watching videos or listening to music.

  • Consider overall sound clarity.
  • Check the audio specifications provided by the manufacturer.
  • Consider whether the headset suits the types of games you play.
  • Look at any advertised audio features.

4. Microphone

If you play multiplayer games or communicate with other players, the microphone is an important feature to consider.

  • Check whether the headset includes a microphone.
  • Consider microphone positioning and adjustability.
  • Check whether the microphone can be muted.
  • Look for any advertised microphone features.

5. Connection Type

Gaming headsets can use different connection methods. Check which type works best with your gaming setup.

  • Wired connection
  • Wireless connection
  • USB connection
  • 3.5 mm audio connection

Always check the specific product specifications to confirm the connection type and supported devices.

6. Controls

Convenient controls can make a gaming headset easier to use during a game.

  • Check whether volume controls are included.
  • Check whether microphone mute controls are available.
  • Consider how easily you can reach the controls.
  • Check whether additional controls are provided.

7. Value for Money

Consider the features you actually need instead of choosing a headset based only on appearance or price.

Compare compatibility, comfort, audio quality, microphone features, connection type and controls with the price to see whether the headset matches your needs.

Gaming Headset Quick Checklist

  • ☐ Compatible with your gaming device
  • ☐ Comfortable for long sessions
  • ☐ Suitable audio quality
  • ☐ Useful microphone
  • ☐ Suitable connection type
  • ☐ Convenient controls
  • ☐ Suitable design and fit
  • ☐ Price matches the features
